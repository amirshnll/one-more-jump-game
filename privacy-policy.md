# Privacy Policy for One More Jump

One More Jump does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs entirely within the extension popup without advertising, analytics, or tracking. No data is sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

The extension stores language, sound, and reduced-motion preferences, together with the best distance, highest score, and number of games played. This data is used only to preserve the user's preferences and display personal game records.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is used only to keep game settings and records after the extension is closed. It is not used to access websites, browsing history, personal files, or user accounts, and it does not transmit information over the internet.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
